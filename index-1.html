How to read this scanner

It counts the parity of recent last digits. Even digits are 0, 2, 4, 6, 8; odd digits are 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. “Most frequent” only describes the selected sample — it is not a forecast or a probability that the next digit will match.

Important: Even and odd digits are not reliably predictable from previous digits. Do not treat a higher recent count as a BUY/SELL signal. Practise on demo and never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
